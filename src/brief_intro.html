Lixiao Cui is an assistant professor of College of Computer Science, Nankai University. He received the B.E. and Ph.D. degree from Nankai University in 2019 and 2025. His research interests focus on storage systems, index structures and AI agent systems. His papers have been published in major conferences and journals, including FAST, SIGMOD, ICDE, TKDE, TOS, TACO, TPDS etc.

Recruitment: Our research group is looking for proactive PhD students, Master students, and Research Assistants (RAs). If you are interested in research on storage systems and machine learning systems, please feel free to contact me.
